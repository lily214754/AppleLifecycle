Bud development (BBCH 01,03,07,09) Occurs in spring, beginning just before inflorescence emergence. This phase continues for 24 days and concludes shortly after end of inflorescence emergence, around mid-flowering
cite{kurokuraRegulationSeasonalFlowering2013, martinezPhenologicalGrowthStages2019}		 Bud development begins with visibly swollen buds that feature elongated bud scales and light-colored patches (BBCH 01). The process progresses as bud scales become lighter and densely covered with hairs (BBCH 03) for vegetative buds. Bud break is marked by the appearance of green leaf tips (BBCH 07) and continues growing until these tips extend about 5 mm above the bud scales (BBCH 09) cite{meierGrowthStagesMonoand1997}. Vegetative buds develop into leafy shoots, with true leaves forming the leaves of the shoot or cluster, while bud scales and transitional leaves abscise cite{verheijMorphologicalPhysiologicalAspects1996}.

Bud break number: Averagely range 10 to 35 buds per 100cm shoots length cite{labuschagneSelectionIncreasedBudbreak2003}
Starch content: Decrease in both fine and coarse roots starting from bud break.

Leaf development (BBCH 10,11,15,19) Begins before bud break and continue for around from 45 days to two months to all leaves completely unfolded and expanded
cite{loescherCarbohydrateReservesTranslocation1990, wunscheRelationshipLeafArea2000}
cite{loescherCarbohydrateReservesTranslocation1990, martinezPhenologicalGrowthStages2019}		 Leaf tip growth and unfolding leaf expansion significantly increase leaf area, influenced by factors including weather, apple genetics, training systems, canopy height
cite{barrittCultivarCanopyPosition1990, barrittLightLevelInfluences1991, laksoLeafAreaDevelopment1984, meierGrowthStagesMonoand1997}.

Canopy growth begins with a slight increase until bloom, followed by a rapid expansion that peaks around two months after bud break and concludes mid-season
cite{wunscheBasesProductivityApple1996}. The initial peak in total leaf area, just before bloom, is due to the increase in spur leaves and continues for about one-month post-bloom. The second peak occurs approximately two weeks after bloom due to shoot leaves and completes around two months post-bloom
cite{barrittLightLevelInfluences1991, forsheyRelationshipVegetativeGrowth1989, laksoLeafAreaDevelopment1984}.

Leaf development progresses as fruiting spur primary leaves complete before bourse leaves and vegetative spur leaves cite{barrittLightLevelInfluences1991, laksoLeafAreaDevelopment1984}.

Inflorescence emergence (BBCH 51 - 59) Begins prior to vegetative bud development, lasting for 28 days and concluding before vegetative development ends cite{martinezPhenologicalGrowthStages2019}. From 1969 to 2018, the timing of floral bud swelling and bud burst has advanced by 13.8 and 14.8 days, respectively cite{chituTimingPhenologicalStages2020}. The floral bud development starts with swollen buds that have elongated scales and light-colored patches (BBCH 51). As the buds swell, the scales lighten and grow hairy (BBCH 52). Bud burst occurs with the emergence of green leaf tips and visible flowers (BBCH 53), followed by the pink stage where petals elongate and sepals begin to open (BBCH 57). The stage culminates with most flowers forming a hollow ball (BBCH 59) cite{meierGrowthStagesMonoand1997}. Image

Flowering (BBCH 60 - 69) Occurs in spring after flower emergence cite{kurokuraRegulationSeasonalFlowering2013, milyaevProfilingPhytohormonesApple2022, verheijMorphologicalPhysiologicalAspects1996} and flower in a staggered manner cite{martinezPhenologicalGrowthStages2019}, with the flowering date varying by more than 30 days cite{liebhardMappingQuantitativePhysiological2003}. The duration lasts for several weeks and most cultivars flower for about 3 weeks cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003, p. 539}. In the northern hemisphere, Full blooming usually takes place between the second and third week of April in Northern Hemisphere cite{martinezPhenologicalGrowthStages2019}.

From 1969 to 2018, the timing of beginning of flowering and end of flowering advanced by 10.7 and 7.3 days, respectively cite{chituTimingPhenologicalStages2020}.

The influence of proximity to the ocean lead to 10 days advances in continental and 6-7 days advances in oceanic cite{legaveComprehensiveOverviewSpatial2013} Warm winter and insufficient winter chilling can delay flowering time cite{campoyDormancyTemperateFruit2011, petriConsequencesInsufficientWinter2003}. Shorter flowering periods in temperate conditions and longer periods in mild conditions cite{legaveDifferentiatedResponsesApple2015}.
