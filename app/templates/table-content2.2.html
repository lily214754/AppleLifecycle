Objective Suggested Operations for Orchard Management
- Increase fruit size and quality
- Increase return bloom
- Control crop load
- Reduce incident of biennial bearing 		Thinning:
Control crop load at 4-6 fruits per square cm trunk cross-sectional area (TCSA) depending on variety; No crop in second year for lower density orchard cite{robinsonCropLoadManagement2008}.
Control the growth of canopy to fill allotted space Pruning & Training:
Manage leader development by removing competing shoots and new shoots below the leader when they reach 2-5 cm, but not recommended for low-vigour trees cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003}; Bending, scoring and ringing can be applied to regulate tree growth if flowering is postponed cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003};
Control shoots growth of leader shoot with an incremental increase: 12-24 inches in first year, 30-36 inches in second and third years, and 18 inches in fourth year cite{robinsonCropLoadManagement2008}.
Provide appropriate nutrition to support vigorous tree growth and increase fruit quality Fertilization:
Apply before bud break in early spring based on soil test cite{howFertilizeApple2023}; Recommendations available via guide cite{2023IntegratedOrchard2023}.
Modify tree growth and fruit characteristic Plant growth regulator:
According to goals, practical recommendations during different stages via cite{2023IntegratedOrchard2023}.
Optimal usage of water resources to supply adequate water, maintain suitable soil moisture level to avoid tree stress Irrigation schedule:
The schedule is based on thresholds or indexes determined by the method or simulation model specific to field, accounting for weather, soil and plant status cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003};
Refer to cite{bolandGuideBestPractice2002} for maintaining soil moisture tension between 8 and 40 kPa, except dormancy period. In drought conditions, it is advisable to irrigate even in winter cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003}.
- Control weeds and alleviate the competition between tree and other plants
- Prevent soil erosion, improve water infiltration and nutrient retention 		Orchard Floor Management:
Use mulching or herbicides to eliminate weeds cite{brightOrchardPlantProtection2006};
Up to six cultivation per season may be required to suppress weeds cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003};
Herbicides spraying require 200-400l per ha of material to be effective and the water volume varies with the herbicide type cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003};
Refer to cite{2023IntegratedOrchardManagement} for practical usage of various herbicides.
- Reduce sunburn, enhance fruit quality
- Protect against damage from birds and extreme weather condition 		Light Management:
Use reflective films and shading such as coloured net cite{bastiasLightQualityManagement2012}; Bagging is also a common management cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003}.
Monitor the status of tree to apply intervention for controlling pest and diseases, ensuring tree health Integrated Pest and Disease Management:
Refer to the strategy outlined in the manual by cite{hetheringtonIntegratedPestDisease2005} of monitoring duration and frequency, symptom described and appropriate action can perform. According to goals, practical recommendations during different stages via cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003}.
