Purpose Operation
- Manage soil physical condition for optimal tree growth
- Reduce erosion and enhance water conservation 		Modify Landscape:
- Levelling: Involves adding topsoil to flatter or uniformly shaped ground
- Contouring: Establish the orchard parallel to natural contour of the land
- Terracing: Create stepped ground only considered when other soil and water conservation methods are insufficient
- Subsoil: Perform subsoiling parallel and perpendicular to the position of tree rows cite{parkerHighDensityApple1998}
- Modifying Drainage: Find practical information in the article cite{schwabSoilWaterConservation1982}
Manage soil chemical component for optimal tree growth Apply Material to Adjust Soil Condition:
- Liming/Acidifying materials: Adjust soil pH using liming material for neutralization and material like elemental sulfur for acidification, based on soil test result. Common liming materials are listed in cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003}
- Organic material: Apply material such as hay, straw, woodchips and manure at an effective rate of 30-60 t/ha and mix them into root zone depth; application post-planting on the surface also feasible cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003}
- Fertilizer: Apply nutrient like phosphorous and potassium based on soil test, as outlined from cite{2023IntegratedOrchard2023}
- Maintain soil infiltration capacity and aeration
- Mix pre-plant amendments and fertilizer
- Control weeds and improve root growth
cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003} 		Perform Tillage:
- Ploughing: Typically executed to about 20 cm depth below ground. Deeper ploughing up to 35-40 cm is possible, but not recommended cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003}
Enhance specific apple-replant disease control Fumigate: Apply chemical biocides if needed, especially for replant sites cite{parkerHighDensityApple1998}. For some successful treatment, refer to cite{ferreeApplesBotanyProduction2003}
- Alleviate tree water stress to maintain health, especially in high density orchard
cite{jiangInvestigationEffectiveIrrigation2021} 		Install an Irrigation System: Utilize drippers, sprinklers, or pipes, depending on orchard requirements cite{koechImprovingIrrigationWater2018, petersDripIrrigationAgricultural2015}
