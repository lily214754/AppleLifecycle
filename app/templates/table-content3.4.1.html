 Nutrient (g/kg):
- Iron (Fe): varies significantly, ranging from 100 to 1200
There is a rapid decrease from full bloom to 3 weeks after full bloom (WAFB), followed by a slow and continuous decline until 10 WAFB, stabilizing towards the end of fruit maturation.
- Nitrogen(N): 24 in ‘Golden Delicious’, 17 in ‘Gala’, and 15 in ‘Fuji’, and decreases to 8, then to 4.
- Boron (B): 37 in ‘Golden Delicious’, 32 in ‘Gala’, and 30 in ‘Fuji’, reducing to 25, then to approximately 12.
- Phosphorus (P): Decreases from 4.5 to 1, then to about 0.5.
- Potassium (K): Reduces from 22 to about 10, then to 4.
- Cupper (Cu): Starts at approximately 30, declines to 10, then to 5.
- Calcium (Ca): 4 in ‘Golden Delicious’, 6.8 in ‘Gala’, and 7 in ‘Fuji’, decreases to 1, then to about 0.1.
- Magnesium (Mg): Reduces from about 4 to 1, then to about 0.1.

- Manganese (Mn): Begins at 35 in ‘Golden Delicious’, 55 in ‘Gala’, and 15 in ‘Fuji’, declining to about 20, then to 8.
- Zinc (Zn): Starts at 55 in ‘Golden Delicious’, 40 in ‘Gala’, and 25 in ‘Fuji’, decreasing to about 10, then to 0.1.
- Zinc (Zn): 55 (‘Golden delicious’), 40(‘Gala’) and 25(‘Fuji’) -> ~10 -> ~0.1
(Nachtigall and Dechen, 2006)
