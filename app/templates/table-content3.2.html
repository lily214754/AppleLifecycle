LAI in training system
Spur LAI:
- Bud break to full bloom: 0.0 to 0.2
- Full bloom to ~2 months post-bud break: 0.25 to 2.06 (Y/M.26), 0.25 to 1.08-1.25 (SL/M.9, CL/9/111, CL/M.7)
Shoot LAI:
- Bud break to full bloom: 0.0 to 0.1
- Full bloom to ~2 months post-bud break: 0.1 to ~0.7 (Y/M.26), 0.1 to ~0.9 (SL/M.9), 0.1 to ~0.6 (CL/9/111), 0.1 to ~0.3 (CL/M.7)
Total LAI:
- Bud break to full bloom: 0.0 to 0.5
- Full bloom to ~2 months post-bud break: 0.5 to 3.6 (Y/M.26), 0.5 to 2.6 (SL/M.9), 0.5 to 2.2 (CL/9/111), 0.5 to 1.7 (CL/M.7)

Leaf area per tree (square m):
Spur Leaf Area:
- Bud Break to Full Bloom: Increase from 0 to 2.5

- Full Bloom to 1 Month Post: Increase from 3 to 28

Leaf area per spur (square cm):
- Bud Break to Full Bloom: Ranges from 50 to 90 for vegetative spurs and 50 to 70 for fruiting spurs.
- Full Bloom to 1 Month After: Increases to 90 to 350 for vegetative spurs, 70 to 200 for fruiting spurs.
cite{barrittLightLevelInfluences1991}
Leaf number per spur:
1 Week Before Full Bloom to 3 Months After: Increases from 4 to 14 for ‘Delicious’, 5 to 15 for ‘Rome’, and 6 to 20 for ‘Granny Smith’.
cite{barrittCultivarCanopyPosition1990}

Mean leaf area (square cm):
For 'Empire'/M7 Variety
Spur leaves:
- Bud break to full bloom: Increase from ~10 to ~35 for both vegetative and flowering
- Full bloom to fruit drop: Increase from ~35 to ~70 for vegetative, ~35 to ~60 for flowering
- Fruit drop to 1 month post fruit drop: ~70 to ~90 for vegetative, ~60 to ~70 for flowering
Terminal shoot leaves:
- Bud break to full bloom: Increase from ~10 to ~35 for both pruned and unpruned
- Full bloom to fruit drop: Increase from ~35 to ~210 for pruned tree, ~35 to ~190 square cm for unpruned tree
- Fruit drop to 1 month post fruit drop: Increase from ~210 to ~325 for pruned tree, ~190 to ~275 for unpruned tree
cite{laksoLeafAreaDevelopment1984}

Leaf dry weight in canopy height (mg per square cm)
1 Week Before Full Bloom to 1 Week After:
Decline from approximately 9 to 5 for both 1m and 2m canopy height
Post Full Bloom to Harvest:
Gradual increase from approximately 5 to 7 for 1m canopy
Gradual increase from approximately 5 to 9 for 2m canopy
cite{barrittCultivarCanopyPosition1990}

Leaf photosynthetic rate (carbon dioxide per dm square per hr ): Increase 5-fold, peaking when leaf plastochron index (LPI) is between 10-12, then decreases to an average value of 25-30
Leaf resistance (sec per cm): Decrease 3-4-fold from 10 to 2.5 during early leaf development, increase slight at a leaf plastochron index (LPI) of 10-12 and then decreased gradually
Stomatal resistance (sec per cm): Decrease 3-4-fold from 5.5 to 1.5 during early leaf development, slight increase at LPI 10-12, followed by a gradual decrease.
Leaf transpiration ratio (mg H2O lost/mg CO2 fixed/dm2 hr): Decrease 5-fold during early leaf development, reaching a low of 55 at LPI 11
Leaf chlorophyll: Gradually increases up to LPI 10 and remains stable thereafter.
cite{kennedyChangesPhotosyntheticCharacteristics1981}
