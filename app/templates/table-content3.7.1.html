Decreases from 14 at commercial harvest to 4.76 one week later for 'Braeburn' \cite{tartachnykEffectDelayedFruit2004}

Declines from a range of 0.8 to 1.1 at harvest to below 0.2 two months after harvest for 'Braeburn' \cite{tartachnykEffectDelayedFruit2004}

Stable with value 0.80 for three weeks after commercial harvest and decrease to 0.76 \cite{tartachnykEffectDelayedFruit2004}
