Pollination Occur during overlapping flowering duration between fruit-producing trees and pollinating trees [1] and optimal pollination should occur from the early flowering stage, when approximately 30% of flowers are open, to the full flowering when more than 50% of flowers open completely [89]. The compatible pollen produced by the stamens in the pollinators is transferred by pollinators (insects, e.g. honeybees) to pollinate the stigmas of tree flowers [1, 90, 44]. - Temperature

Fertilization The time from pollination to fertilization ranges from 48 hours to 13 days [91] and the interval for fertilization happens, which is the effective pollination period (EPP) varies from 2–9 days depending on cultivar, tree condition, and temperature [1]. Pollen grains deposited on the stigma germinate and growth of pollen-tube reach to receptive ovule. The nuclei then travel through the pollen tube unite with the egg cell and polar nuclei, producing the diploid zygote and a triploid nucleus, respectively. In contrast, flowers without fertilization simply soon abscise [1]. - Temperature

Fruit set Typically occurs soon after fertilization [1]. 7 days after pollination for 'Golden Delicious' [69]. when more than 95% of the petals fell was recorded as the date of fruit set. The fertilized ovary grows with surrounding receptacle tissues to form fruit set, indicating the transition from flower to fruit set [1, 64]. - Microscopy / bud dissection
