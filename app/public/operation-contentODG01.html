Orchard management strategies for young apple trees

Objective Suggested Operations
- Increase fruit size and quality
- Increase return bloom
- Control crop load
- Reduce incident of biennial bearing
- Control the growth of canopy to fill allotted space
- Provide appropriate nutrition to support vigorous tree growth and increase fruit quality
- Modify tree growth and fruit characteristic
- Optimal usage of water resources to supply adequate water
- Maintain suitable soil moisture level to avoid tree stress
- Control weeds and alleviate the competition between tree and other plants
- Prevent soil erosion, improve water infiltration and nutrient retention
- Reduce sunburn, enhance fruit quality
- Protect against damage from birds and extreme weather condition
- Monitor the status of tree to apply intervention for controlling pest and diseases, ensuring tree health
